The Craft

The Art of Seeing What Others Miss

At ForeShadow Production , we believe every wedding is more than an event — it’s a beautiful story of love, emotions, and unforgettable moments. Our passion lies in capturing those stories through both photography and cinematic videography, ensuring every smile, tear, and heartfelt glance is preserved forever. ”

From candid shots to grand cinematic films, our work blends creativity with authenticity, giving you a perfect balance of timeless elegance and natural moments. Whether it’s the sparkle in your eyes during the vows or the joy of your family dancing, we strive to capture the soul of your celebration. With years of experience, state-of-the-art equipment, and a deep love for storytelling, “We specialize in turning your once-in-a-lifetime moments into timeless memories, blending art with emotions.

The Team

THE CREATIVE COLLECTIVE

The directors, documentarians, and visual artists who paint your stories.

SIDDHARTH SHARMA

Founder & Lead Photographer

With over a decade of documenting celebrations around the globe, Siddharth blends fine art editorial portraiture with raw photojournalism. His work has been recognized globally for its soft natural light play and emotional resonance.

REVA SHAH

Co-Founder & Creative Director

Reva directs the overall visual style and narrative design of all ForeShadow wedding films and books. Coming from a fine-art background, she designs bespoke atmospheres, coordinates palettes, and ensures each couple's personality is woven into a tailored visual narrative.

AMAN GUPTA

Principal Cinematographer

Aman focuses on high-end motion composition and cinematic framing. His signature filming style relies on intimate angles, rich colors, and slow-paced documentation that elevates the real emotion of a wedding day into a breathtaking cinema experience.
