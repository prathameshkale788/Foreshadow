PRATHAMESH KALE

I’m Prathamesh Kale, founder of ForeShadow, born on 11 October 2001 in the small village of Sanglood, Akola, Maharashtra.

Growing up in a small village, I always dreamed of creating cinema that could move people. What started as a passion for cameras gradually became a lifelong pursuit of storytelling through weddings, documentaries, and films.

Today, I work across India, documenting love stories, cultures, traditions, and real human emotions. Every project, whether it’s a wedding, documentary, or cinematic film, is approached with the same belief: every story deserves to be told with honesty, emotion, and purpose.

For me, filmmaking is more than a profession—it’s a way of preserving moments that become memories for generations.

2018

I started my photography journey in 2018, shooting with a rented camera. From model and portfolio shoots to every opportunity I got, each project helped me learn, grow, and build the foundation of my career.

2019-2020

In 2019, I stepped into wedding photography. By 2020, I was getting consistent bookings, my team started growing, and on my birthday, I bought my first PC—a milestone that took my work to the next level.

2022-2024

On December 6, 2022, I bought my first cinema camera, the Sony FX3. In 2023, I started working with premium wedding and commercial clients. Throughout 2024, I collaborated with celebrities and completed many remarkable projects.

I started my photography journey in 2018, shooting with a rented camera. From model and portfolio shoots to every opportunity I got, each project helped me learn, grow, and build the foundation of my career.

2021

On March 23, 2021, I bought my first camera, the Sony A7 III. That marked my shift toward cinematography, where I focused on creating films while continuing to edit my own work. The biggest turning point came in October 2021, when I moved to Pune to chase bigger opportunities.

2025

On October 11, 2025, I took the biggest step of my journey by launching my own production house. I separated my commercial and wedding work, marking the beginning of Fourshadow—a brand built to tell timeless wedding stories.
